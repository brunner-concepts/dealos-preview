DealOS Architecture Reflection Rubric

2026-06-15.v1
Mohammed's Response

DealOS should keep borrower material inside a controlled deployment because private-credit work contains sensitive company data, confidential metrics, and investment judgment. Static pages can show the interface, but extraction, storage, model calls, and grading need private runtime controls. Network isolation, encryption, least privilege, citations, audit logs, and human review reduce the chance that a generated deck or recommendation leaves without source support or approval. The tradeoff is more operational complexity, but the failure mode I would watch is a browser or worker path bypassing policy and exposing unsupported outputs.
