Layer-by-Layer Lesson

Read each row as an architectural responsibility and a privacy rule.

9 stages
01

Inputs

Connectors

Sponsor memos, data-room files, emails, meeting notes, models, and CRM records enter through governed ingestion.

Keep raw material in private storage with tenant-scoped access.
02

Deal Intelligence Layer

Orchestrator

The orchestration layer routes tasks to workers, retrieval, model adapters, queues, and policies.

Send the smallest permitted context to the approved runtime.
03

Extraction

Document workers

Deal terms, covenants, metrics, risks, and open questions become structured records with confidence.

Separate raw evidence from normalized fields and flag uncertainty.
04

Memory

Tenant data plane

Approved context, embeddings, decisions, and prior analyst notes become reusable private memory.

Store memory per tenant and respect retention, deletion, and role boundaries.
05

Reconciliation

Validation workers

Figures and claims are tied out across PDFs, spreadsheets, emails, and previous approvals.

Do not promote conflicting facts until exceptions are reviewed.
06

Citations

Evidence service

Every generated sentence, number, and recommendation can point back to a durable source anchor.

No citation, no committee-ready claim.
07

Policy Engine

Governance service

The policy engine evaluates user role, provider, destination, data class, and approval state.

Block model calls, exports, or syncs that violate deployment policy.
08

Human Approval

Analyst workflow

Analysts approve exceptions, external delivery, and controlled syncs with a recorded rationale.

Use human approval for irreversible movement or high-impact decisions.
09

Outputs

Delivery layer

IC memos, credit decks, audit packets, Anaplan payloads, and CRM updates are produced from governed state.

Export only approved, cited, policy-compliant material.

Deployment Controls

Controls Mohammed should ask for before production.

Required

Self-hosted or VPC

Control

Run workers, queues, storage, and model gateways inside Mohammed's chosen environment.

Bring your own keys

Control

Provider credentials, KMS, and HSM controls remain tenant-operated.

No training egress

Control

Prompts, files, extracted facts, and memory are not reused outside the deployment boundary.

Immutable audit trail

Control

Log citations, policy decisions, approvals, exports, syncs, and overrides.

Approval gate test

If an action changes an external system, sends data to a model outside the approved boundary, or produces an IC artifact, it should pass policy and human approval first.

The shortest version

Private inputs become trusted outputs only after extraction, memory, reconciliation, citations, policy, and approval agree.
